Research

Published

Design of a UWB PDOA Auto-Following Shopping Cart System for Complex Supermarket Environments

Published in Electronic Technology & Software Engineering

This paper presents an auto-following shopping cart designed for complex supermarket environments. The system uses Ultra-Wideband Phase Difference of Arrival (UWB PDOA) ranging and positioning to track a shopper in real time through aisles and crowds, reducing staff workload and smoothing the checkout flow. Field tests validated positioning accuracy and robustness in cluttered indoor scenes.

CCIR 2026

Individual Valuation vs. Collective Preference: The K-pop Photocard Market as a Collective Trap

2026 CCIR Summer Research Paper

This paper examines the K-pop photocard market as a case where collective preference diverges from individual valuation. Using price, trading, and survey data, it argues that speculative dynamics can lock participants into a collectively suboptimal equilibrium, offering an empirical view of how markets amplify group behavior over individual rationality.
